# Facemesh

**See yourself rebuilt from thousands of glowing dots, in 3D, live.**

Facemesh turns your camera into a moving dot portrait. Your face, your hands
and your body are drawn with dots that follow every move you make. Spin the
picture around with your finger to see it in 3D, change its colours, and
record short videos with your voice and your favourite song playing softly
in the background.

Everything happens on your own phone. Nothing you film is sent anywhere.

**Open the app:** https://lister2005.github.io/facemesh-app/

---

## Getting started

1. Open the link above in **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Tap **Start camera** and allow the camera when your phone asks.
3. Hold the phone so your face is in view. The dots gather and form your picture.

The first visit takes a little while, because the app downloads about 20 MB.
After that it opens quickly and even works without internet.

**Keep it on your home screen:** in Chrome, open the menu (⋮) and choose
**Add to Home screen**. Facemesh then opens full screen like any other app.

Just want a look first? Tap **Try the demo** to play with a spinning ball of
dots without using the camera.

---

## Moving the picture

| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| Drag with one finger | Turn the picture around to see it from the side |
| Pinch with two fingers | Make the 3D effect deeper or flatter |
| Double-tap | Put the picture back to the front |

You can also turn it **without touching the screen**: in front of the camera,
touch the tip of your thumb to the tip of your index finger (like picking up
a grain of rice), keep them together and move your hand. Open your fingers to
let go.

## The buttons

Along the bottom:

- **Colour** – show your real colours instead of one colour.
- **Palette** – switch between green, amber, ice blue, white, and a "depth"
  look where close things are red and far things are blue.
- **● Rec** – start and stop recording a video.
- **Shot** – save a picture.
- **Flip** – switch between the front and back camera.
- **More** – everything else (see below).

Under **More**:

- **ASCII** – draw you with letters and symbols instead of dots.
- **Glow** – a soft glow around the dots.
- **Trails** – moving dots leave a short trail behind them.
- **Background** – show the room behind you as faint dots too.
- **Orbit** – the picture slowly sways from side to side by itself.
- **Reset view**, **Depth −**, **Depth +** – the same as the finger moves above.
- **Gestures** – turn the hand gestures on or off.
- **Palm scatter** – when on, hold an open hand still in front of the camera
  for a moment and the dots burst apart. Close your hand and they come back.
- **Quick follow** – helps your face keep up when you move quickly.
- **Voice ripple** – the dots ripple out from your face when you talk.
- **Fullscreen** and **Help**.

The app remembers your choices for next time.

## Recording videos with sound

Tap **● Rec** to start and tap it again to stop. The video is saved to your
phone's downloads.

Your voice is recorded with the video. You can also add a song that plays
quietly underneath:

1. Tap **More**, then **Choose song…**, and pick any song on your phone.
2. Record as usual. The song plays softly in the background and starts again
   if your video is longer than the song.

Use the **Microphone** and **Song** buttons under More to leave either one out.
Your chosen song is remembered.

## Tips for the best picture

- **Light your face.** A lamp or a window in front of you makes a big difference.
- **Stay about an arm's length away**, with your head and shoulders in view.
- **A plain background** helps the app tell you apart from the room.
- If it feels slow, close other apps and try the back camera, which is often sharper.

---

## If something isn't working

- **The camera won't start:** make sure you allowed the camera. In Chrome, tap
  the icon next to the address and turn the camera on, then reload the page.
- **Nothing appears:** check that your face is in view and there is enough light.
- **The video won't play:** some phones save videos in a format their gallery
  doesn't open. Try playing it in Chrome or in a video player app.
- **It works best in Chrome.** Other browsers may not support everything.

On a computer, open the same link in Chrome and press **?** to see the
keyboard shortcuts.
